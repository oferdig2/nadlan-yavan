Nadlan KAEK Importer - Mac
==========================

Needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer. Take the package that matches your Mac:
- "mac-apple-silicon" for M1/M2/M3/M4 Macs (Apple menu > About This Mac shows "Chip: Apple M...")
- "mac-intel" for Macs with an Intel processor

Install
1. Double-click the downloaded .tar.gz; it unpacks to "Nadlan KAEK Importer.app".
2. Drag "Nadlan KAEK Importer.app" into your Applications folder.
3. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the developer:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer: click "Done", then open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll down to "Nadlan KAEK Importer was blocked" and click "Open Anyway", then confirm.
   - macOS 13-14: right-click (or Control-click) the app > Open > Open.
   This is needed only once.

It uses Google Chrome if it is installed; otherwise it downloads its own browser the first time (~150 MB).

Use
- Zoom in on the map until the yellow parcel lines show, choose the geographic area in the Nadlan panel,
  then click "Acquire polygons". Or tick "Import each parcel I click on the map" and click parcels yourself.
- Close the browser window to quit.
- A report of every run is saved in Documents/Nadlan/KAEK imports.

Settings
The Nadlan address comes with the package. To change it, create
~/Library/Application Support/Nadlan/importer.json with:
    { "apiUrl": "https://your-nadlan-address" }
Logs: ~/Library/Application Support/Nadlan/logs

Uninstall
Drag "Nadlan KAEK Importer" from Applications to the Bin.
